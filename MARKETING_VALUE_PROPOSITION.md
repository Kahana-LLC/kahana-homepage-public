## 2. One-liner / elevator

**One-liner:**  
Kahana is where creators publish curated digital hubs, get discovered on a real marketplace, and get paid — without stitching together Gumroad, Notion, and link-in-bio tools.

**Elevator (15 seconds):**  
Kahana is building the Amazon of digital products: a marketplace (Explore) where buyers browse curated hubs, and creators host, sell, and grow catalogs with built-in Stripe payments, creator profiles, and Aura — the community’s daily signal for what’s worth noticing.

**North star:**  
Make Kahana the default place to discover, buy, and host curated digital products.

---

## 3. Benefits

### For creators

- Publish expertise as a **hub** (files, notes, resource library) — a real product container, not a loose PDF
- Get **discovered on Explore** — buyers can find you beyond your existing audience
- **Monetize with Stripe** — one-time or subscription unlocks
- **Collaborate** with members inside the hub
- Replace the Notion + Gumroad + Linktree stack with one branded surface + profile link

### For buyers

- **Browse a catalog** of digital products by category, price, and creator
- Open a clear creator storefront / hub, pay, get **instant access** in one account
- Less chasing DMs, dead checkout links, and scattered files

### For the community (Aura)

- Members get a **daily Aura budget** to endorse hubs on Explore
- Aura is **social proof and discovery signal** — “this is worth noticing”
- Quality can rise with **people**, not only ads or SEO
- Streaks encourage habitual participation in the marketplace

**Aura is not cryptocurrency.** Frame it as endorsement / reputation / marketplace culture.

## 4. Value proposition and differentiation

### Positioning statements

**For creators** who package expertise into digital products, **Kahana** is a **marketplace and storefront** that lets them publish curated hubs, get discovered on Explore, and monetize with Stripe — unlike generic file hosts or link-in-bio tools that lack discovery or built-in commerce.

**For buyers**, Kahana is a **curated digital marketplace** where they find high-signal products in public hubs — unlike scattered PDFs, Notion pages, or one-off Gumroad links with no browse experience.

### Competitive contrast

| Alternative | What it does well | What Kahana adds |
|-------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Gumroad / checkout tools** | Sell a link | Browseable **Explore** marketplace + **hub as the product** |
| **Stan / Linktree** | Profile / link-in-bio | **Category discovery** + richer product container |
| **Notion / Drive** | Docs and files | **Commerce + public discovery** as core, not an afterthought |
| **Social feeds** | Attention | Catalog- and creator-led discovery + **Aura** (not endless scroll) |

### Aura as differentiator

Lead with marketplace + get paid; use Aura as the **culture / engagement layer**.

> Unlike paywall links that die in a feed, Kahana has **Aura** — the community’s daily signal for what’s actually good. Creators earn visibility through quality endorsement; buyers browse a marketplace that ranks with people, not just price tags.

**Press option:**  
“Marketplace, storefront, payments — and Aura, so the community, not just algorithms, lifts the best hubs.”

## 6. You do XYZ steps and get ROI

| You do | You get (ROI) |
|--------|----------------|
| **1. Create a hub** (product, playbook, template pack, resource library) | A professional storefront instead of a loose folder or PDF |
| **2. Publish / list it on Explore** | **Discovery** — buyers can find you without only relying on your existing audience |
| **3. Connect Stripe & turn on monetization** | **Revenue** — one-time or subscription unlocks |
| **4. Share your Kahana profile** (and keep the hub updated) | **Owned funnel** — one link that hosts *and* sells |
| **5. Engage the marketplace** (browse peers; Give / earn Aura) | **Social proof + ranking signal** — Aura shows your work is valued |

### One-breath ROI

**Creator:** Ship once → get found → get paid → get endorsed.  
Time saved vs stitching tools + incremental sales from Explore + proof of demand via purchases and Aura.

**Buyer:** Search once → buy once → access in one place.  
Less friction than chasing DMs and dead links.

### Press-friendly version

> Creators do five things: build a hub, list it, enable payments, share their profile, and show up for the community. The return is discovery, sales, and Aura — marketplace trust you can’t buy with a lonely checkout link.
