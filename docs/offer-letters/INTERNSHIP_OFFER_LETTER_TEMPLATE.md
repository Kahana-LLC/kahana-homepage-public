We are pleased to offer you a position in the **Kahana Learning Internship**, an educational internship program designed for individuals who want to learn and gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced, innovative startup environment.

## Position & Status

- **Role:** Learning Intern  
- **Functional Area(s):** [FUNCTIONAL AREA — e.g., Engineering, Product, Marketing, Sales, Project Management, Design, Finance, or Human Resources]  
- **Status:** You will participate as an intern in an educational capacity. This is not an employment relationship; you are not an employee of Kahana.

## Key Terms

**Compensation:** This is an unpaid, educational internship focused on learning and gaining experience. There is no stipend, salary, or other compensation.

**Schedule & Location:** The internship is flexible and remote. You will work around your schedule—there is no fixed daily duration. You may participate from anywhere; there is no in-office requirement.

**Structure:** You will receive a list of tasks every two weeks with instructions and learning objectives. You will join our intern community via WhatsApp or Slack for collaboration, task assignments, and communication with the team and other interns. You will have access to structured learning plans, mentorship opportunities, and hands-on projects aligned with your functional area.

**Duration:** There is no fixed end date. You may remain in the internship as long as it continues to be a good fit.

## At-Will Agreement

This internship is **at-will**. Either party may end the internship at any time, for any reason, with or without notice.

**To terminate or leave the internship**, you may do so at any time by sending a written email message to your Supervisor, Adam, at **adam@kahana.co**. Kahana may also end the internship at any time.

## No Expectation of Employment

This internship does not lead to and has no impact on obtaining a full-time role at Kahana. There is no expectation of employment before, during, or after the internship. This is a learning experience designed to help you build skills and gain exposure.

## Documentation

You may list this internship experience on your resume and LinkedIn. This offer letter serves as official documentation for your records.
